Simulated Monte Carlo (MC) samples are produced with the {\sc geant4}-based~\cite{geant4} MC package.  Two types of MC samples are used in this analysis as described in the following:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Inclusive MC samples, which includes the production of $\lcp\lcm$ pairs,
          open-charmed mesons, ISR production of vector charmonium(-like) states,
          continuum processes incorporated in {\sc kkmc}~\cite{Jadach2001, Jadach2000}.
          Known decay modes are modeled with EVTGEN~\cite{Lange2001, RongGang2008} using
          the BFs taken from the PDG~\cite{ParticleDataGroup2022}. The remaining unknown
          charmonium decays are modeled with LUNDCHARM~\cite{Jadach2001, Jadach2000}. The
          integrated luminosity is 40 times larger than that of data, and the
          $\ee\to\lcp\lcm$ cross section is based on the analysis of
          Ref.~\cite{WWangMemo2022}. For $\lcp\lcm$ part (denoted as inclusive $\lcp\lcm$
          MC samples), the decay card and the dst files are available in the path
          /besfs5/offline/data/706-1/xyz/mc/*/LcLc2. For non-$\lcp\lcm$ part (denoted as
          hadron MC samples), the samples are available in the path
          /besfs5/offline/data/706-1/xyz/mc/*/hadron. See Ref.~\cite{YJiang_meeting,
              PRLI_meeting} for more details about the generation of official MC samples.
    \item PHSP signal MC samples for the process $\lcp\to p\Ks\piz$ (charge-conjugated
          mode implied) are generated in two configurations:
          \begin{itemize}
              \item \textbf{Inclusive $\lcm$ decay samples} modeling all possible decay channels. These samples are used in the MC integration in the likelihood construction of the amplitude analysis fit where the detection efficiencies are therefore implemented. At each center-of-mass energy point 500K signal MC events are generated.

              \item \textbf{Detector-invisible $\lcm$ decay samples} where final states are simulated as undetectable. This configuration yields a pure signal sample employed for training the Boosted Decision Tree with Gradient Boosting (BDTG) classifier.
          \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
